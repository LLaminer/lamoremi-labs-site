Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

At a glance: In Center It, you can choose a display name to submit with your score. Submitted display names and scores may be visible to other players on the leaderboard. Please use a nickname, not your real name or other personal details.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Lamoremi Labs LLC (“Lamoremi Labs,” “we,” “us”) handles information associated with Center It and our website at lamoremilabs.com. Center It may also be available on third-party platforms, such as itch.io or TikTok, which operate under their own privacy policies.

2. Information associated with playing

  • Leaderboard submissions: When you choose to submit a high score, you enter a display name that is associated with your score. That display name and score can appear on a public leaderboard.
  • Unity Gaming Services: Center It integrates Unity Gaming Services Leaderboards and Authentication. Unity's documentation identifies a Unity Player ID and IP address as data processed for Leaderboards. Unity Authentication supports player identification for score submissions. Depending on implementation, player identity may be anonymous without requiring an email address or sign-in credentials.
  • Technical information: Hosting providers and game distribution platforms may also process IP addresses, browser or device information, access logs, and diagnostics in delivering their services.
  • Platform information: If you play through a third-party platform, that platform may process account, device, or usage information under its own policies. We do not assume that information is shared with Lamoremi Labs.

3. How information is used

We use submitted leaderboard information to record and display rankings and support gameplay. Technical data may be processed for game delivery, reliability, security, and troubleshooting, as applicable.

4. Public leaderboard and sharing

When you submit a score, the display name you chose and your score may be shown to other players. Do not enter personal information in a display name. Unity Gaming Services processes information to provide the leaderboard and authentication features. Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy provides additional information about its data practices. Other hosting and game distribution providers may process information as needed to provide their services.

5. Retention and deletion

Unity states that personal data in its Leaderboards service is retained by default indefinitely, or until the leaderboard is reset; archives, if enabled, may also be retained indefinitely. We may remove scores or request deletion through Unity's supported tools when appropriate. To request review or removal of a leaderboard entry, contact Lamoremi Labs through our Center It publisher page and include the display name and relevant score. We may ask for reasonable details to identify the entry. Deletion of Unity Authentication player records or data controlled by other platforms may require separate actions.

6. Children and display names

Players should not submit real names, contact details, or other identifying information to the leaderboard. If you believe a child's identifying information has been submitted, please contact us so we can review the request. Unity notes that its Leaderboards service is not intended for applications with child users unless verified parental consent has been obtained where required. We are reviewing the game's audience and applicable platform requirements before offering it to children.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control, but no internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and requests

You can play without putting your real name on the leaderboard. For questions about a submission, or to request correction or deletion where possible, use our publisher page to reach us. Additional rights may apply depending on where you live.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our games and services change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
